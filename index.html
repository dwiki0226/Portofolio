Oil & Gas · ExxonMobil
Nov 2025 – Mar 2026

Owner Estimation System

RoleProject Manager
Team10 people
ValueIDR 300M
StatusDelivered

The problem

An owner estimate is the cost baseline a capital project gets judged against. It has to be produced the same way by different engineers, defended in front of finance and procurement, and still make sense to an auditor a year later. That is a governance problem before it is a software problem.

What I did

  • Ran the full cycle — initiation, planning, execution, closure — for a cross-functional team of ten.
  • Designed the estimation workflow and data model covering cost calculation, scenario analysis and approval, so that every number could be traced back to its input.
  • Built a role-based access and approval hierarchy so each function reviewed and signed only what it owned.
  • Held engineering, finance and procurement to one change-control route instead of side agreements.

Result

Delivered inside scope, timeline and budget, with the approval trail and access model the client needed for audit.

02
Oil & Gas
Jun 2025 – Nov 2025

Document Management System

RolePM & Business Analyst
FocusCompliance
StatusDelivered

The problem

Documents that carry legal and safety weight are only as good as the ability to prove who changed what, when, and under whose authority. Storage was the easy half; retention, classification and evidence were the half that mattered.

What I did

  • Designed the workflows for upload, storage, classification and retrieval, with role-based access, version control and audit trails behind each.
  • Defined the document taxonomy and metadata standard — the part everyone skips and then regrets.
  • Wrote the governance policies to meet the client’s data-security and retention obligations.

Result

A document estate that is searchable by metadata rather than by memory, and traceable end to end.

TEDxUINJakarta 2.0

RoleProject Lead
Team117 people
Budget< IDR 100M
StatusDelivered

The problem

A flagship TEDx runs once, on one date, in front of an audience and a licence holder. The committee was 117 volunteers across program, logistics, media and partnerships — unpaid, mostly first-timers, and impossible to manage by authority alone. Thirteen months to a single unmovable deadline.

What I did

  • Directed planning and execution across four divisions, and built the reporting cadence that let me see trouble early instead of on the day.
  • Owned stakeholder communication with speakers, sponsors and internal committees — three groups with different definitions of “ready”.
  • Drove operational readiness: venue, technical setup, run-of-show, and the rehearsals that turn a plan into a delivery.

Result

Ran on the date, in scope, under IDR 100M. Still the largest team I have coordinated, and the clearest lesson I have had in why governance beats heroics.

RoleProject Manager

The problem

Tender opportunities on Indonesia’s public e-procurement portal are public but not usable — scattered across listings, read by hand, missed by deadline. The value was in getting them into one place fast enough to act on.

What I did

Aviation · Garuda Indonesia Training Center
Oct 2023 – Dec 2023

GITC Document Management System

RolePM Intern
UsersInternal team of 10
MethodWaterfall
StatusRolled out

The problem

An internal team was tracking documents through folders and habit. Before proposing a system, someone had to establish that there was a real gap and not just a preference.

What I did

  • Ran a SWOT analysis of the existing process to locate the actual gaps.
  • Modelled the proposed solution in UML and designed a web-based DMS on a Waterfall framework — a fixed, well-understood scope, which was the right call for a three-month internship window.
  • Built in role-based permissions and version control, and checked the design against data-protection requirements.
  • Implemented and rolled it out to the team of ten.

Result

Shipped and in use inside the internship. It is the project that turned me from someone studying information systems into someone delivering them.
